# Batch 10 — Unterwegs & Verkehr · Szenenplan

103 target words. Two are skipped as Swiss (`der Lift`, `das Tram`, see below), so 101
words go into **twelve scenes** of four to twelve words each. The topic is everyday getting
around: the commute, a bike, a car, a train, an airport pickup, a bus, and directions.
**The Ostsee holiday is not in this batch.** Batch 9, Text 14 booked it, and it belongs to
Batch 11 (Reisen & Urlaub). No text here packs, travels to the coast or takes the island
ferry. The batch ends a few days before departure.

The topic's hazard is the **directions recital**: *an der Kreuzung links abbiegen, an der
Ampel die Straße überqueren, durch den Park …*. Here the directions sit in one scene with
something at stake. Bruckner gives them without his glasses, holding the map upside down
and steering by shops that closed years ago (Text 11). `Ampel`/`überqueren` are moved out
of that scene to the bus (Text 10). The vehicle words are spread over things that go wrong:
a cancelled S-Bahn, a new driver at fifty in a seventy zone, a car in the Einfahrt, and a
Sunday shortcut that ends back in the jam.

## Skipped: the two Swiss words

- **`der Lift`** — the CSV prints *"→ D, A: Aufzug"*. In the Goethe list's terms *Lift* is
  the Swiss word, and the house has said *Aufzug* since Batch 1. The ledger shows the entry
  only because the headword line itself has no region tag.
- **`das Tram`** — *"→ D, A: Straßenbahn"*. The neuter *das Tram* is Swiss. Text 1 says
  *Straßenbahn*.

## Sense decisions made against the CSV, not against the corpus

- **`der Gang` is a gear** (CSV sense 2, *"Schalten Sie … in den dritten Gang"*). Batch 5
  used the corridor sense. Text 3: *"Der erste Gang klemmt ein bisschen."*
- **`die Maschine` is the aircraft** (CSV sense 2, *"Unsere Maschine soll um 7 Uhr 10
  starten"*). Text 6.
- **`der Wagen` is a railway carriage** (CSV sense 2, *"Im zweiten Wagen befindet sich das
  Zugrestaurant"*). This keeps it apart from `Auto` and `Fahrzeug`. Text 9: the three
  carriages of Bruckner's model train.
- **`der Schalter` is a counter staffed by people** (CSV sense 2). Text 6: Tobi asks at the
  airport desk. It is never a light switch, which keeps it away from `Stecker`/`Steckdose`.
- **`die Eisenbahn` is a model railway** (*"eine elektrische Eisenbahn"*, CSV). Text 9.
- **`der Anschluss` is a train connection** (CSV sense 1), not a phone line. Text 1.
- **`umgehen` is *mit etwas umgehen können*** (*"Er kann sehr gut mit Kindern umgehen"*).
  Text 3: you have to know how to handle Tobi's car. It is never "to avoid".
- **`die Spur` is a lane** (*"auf der linken Spur"*, CSV). In the corpus it means a clue.
- **`der Laster` is the lorry** (masculine, CSV: *"Auf der Autobahn waren so viele
  Laster"*). It is not *das Laster* (vice).
- **`die Zone` is a fare zone** (*"nur eine Zone"*, Text 10). The CSV's example is the
  compound *Fußgängerzone*, which credits nothing. *Fußgängerzone* is its own target in
  Text 11.
- **`die Rundfahrt` is a city sightseeing tour** by bus (Text 11). The CSV's harbour tour
  belongs to Batch 11's coast.
- **`die Fähre` is a river ferry** for cyclists on a local Sunday ride (Text 12), not the
  island ferry.
- **`städtisch`**: *ein städtisches Fahrrad*, the city's rental bike (Text 12).
- **`ein-`** is the pronoun *eins* (CSV: *"Willst du auch eins?"*). Text 12: *"Warum soll
  ich eins kaufen?"*
- **`runterwerfen`** is the CSV's thrown key (*"Kannst du mir bitte den Schlüssel
  runterwerfen?"*). It fits a fourth floor without a lift (Text 7).

## Scene order is ownership order

The scanner records a word at its **first** sighting. These avoidances cost something:

- **Texts 1–10 never say bare `Weg`, `Stadt`, `Park` or `links`** (Text 11). Text 4 says
  *linke* and never *links*: *links* would credit `link-` as well. Text 5's brake is not
  *vorne links*.
- **Texts 1–11 never say `Fahrrad`** (Text 12). Julia's bike is *das Rad* in Text 2, and
  Text 7's courtyard has no bike in it.
- **Texts 1–8 never say bare `Zug`** (Text 9), and **Texts 1–5 never say bare `Bahn`**
  (Text 6).
- **Texts 1–4 never say `Motor`** (Text 5). Jule's car stalls in no line.
- **Texts 1–6 never say `parken` or capitalised `Parken`.** *Parken* credits both
  `parken` and `der Park`. Text 6 pays *fürs Parkhaus*, and the scanner does not credit
  that compound.
- **Texts 1–7 never say `Tank`** (Text 8, which owns `tanken`), and **Texts 1–5 never say
  `Land`** (it would credit `landen`).
- **Separable verbs are matched as two tokens anywhere in one utterance.** So no early
  utterance pairs *geh…* with *um* (`umgehen`, Text 3), or *fahr…* with *ab* (`abfahren`,
  Text 9). Text 5 does not say the tyres are *abgefahren*. Text 1's *"Ist es auch"*
  replaced a *"Geht es auch"*, which would have given `umgehen` to Text 1 through the
  same line's *steigen … um*.
- **Texts 1–9 never say bare `Bus`** (Text 10). Text 2's *Busse* is not credited.

Scanner artefacts, fixed by hand after `scan --apply`:

- **`die Fähre`.** Folded, *Fähre* is *fahre*, so every capitalised *Fahrer*, *Fahrt* or
  *Fahren* matches it. Text 10's *Fahrer* is a target there and cannot go, and Text 7's
  *"Fahren Sie den Kleinen …"* comes earlier still. The scan row for `die Fähre` at Text 7
  comes back on every rescan and is ignored. The word is
  recorded with `vocab.py use --batch 10 --text 12 "die Fähre"`, and its card is cut from
  Text 12: *"Jetzt geht es nur noch abwärts, bis runter zur Fähre."*
- **`ein-`** is credited by any *ein/eine/eins*, so the scan logs it at Text 1 (*Gleis
  eins*). Its card is cut from Text 12's pronoun: *"Warum soll ich eins kaufen, Julia?"*
- **`link-`** is credited by *linke* in Text 4, which is also its card line: *"Die linke
  Spur ist ganz frei."*
- **`abfahren`** is credited by *Abfahrt*, and Text 9 also has *"der Zug fährt ab"*. The
  card line for `abfahren` is that one.

### Scene 1 — Gleis drei

- **Premise:** A Monday at 7:40, an S-Bahn platform. Tobi stayed over, and both are on
  their way to work.
- **Angle:** Julia sold Tobi on public transport, and on the first day the S-Bahn is
  cancelled. Tobi turns it into a race, with the loser cooking. Julia then realises that
  winning means eating Tobi's cooking.
- **Words (10):** die S-Bahn, ausfallen, das Gleis, umsteigen, der Anschluss, die U-Bahn, die Straßenbahn, das Verkehrsmittel, das Auto, fahren
- **Chunks:** *"In Ulm müssen Sie umsteigen"* (CSV), *"In Mannheim haben Sie Anschluss"*
  (CSV), *"mit öffentlichen Verkehrsmitteln fahren"* (CSV), `Auto(Akk.) fahren` (3839)
- **Deliberately absent:** `Zug` (9), `Bahn` (6), `Fahrplan` (9), `laufen` (2)

### Scene 2 — Auf dem Gehsteig

- **Premise:** Wednesday morning in front of the house. Julia is cycling to work on the
  pavement, and Frau Wolf, on foot with her shopping bag, will not move aside.
- **Angle:** Frau Wolf lectures a stranger on the rules, finds out it is Julia, and hangs
  her bag on the bike. She then walks faster than Julia can push it.
- **Words (8):** der Fußgänger / die Fußgängerin, der Gehsteig, das Rad, der Radfahrer, die Fahrbahn, der Verkehr, schieben, laufen
- **Chunks:** *"Das Fahrradfahren auf dem Gehsteig ist nicht erlaubt"* (CSV), *"Morgens ist
  hier immer viel Verkehr"* (CSV), *"Ich musste es nach Hause schieben"* (CSV)
- **Deliberately absent:** `Fahrrad` (12), `Straße` (7)

### Scene 3 — Seit Dienstag
