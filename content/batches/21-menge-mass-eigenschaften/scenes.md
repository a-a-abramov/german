# Batch 21 — Menge, Maß & Eigenschaften · Szenenplan

133 target words, **fifteen scenes**, six to eleven words each. Almost all of them are
adjectives and quantities, so every scene is built around something that has to be
**measured, compared or judged**: a cake contest in the courtyard, a recipe scaled up for
forty, a sofa that is ten centimetres too wide for the stairs, a cat put through an
intelligence test, a flea-market locomotive valued by a mechanic. The story is July of
the fourth year: Herr Neumann's first **Hoffest** with a **Kuchenwettbewerb** (Texts 1–10),
then the hot second half of the month (Texts 11–15).

The batch's hazard is the **judging inventory**: a jury scene that hands out one praise
word per turn. The praise and intensifier words are therefore spread one or two per text
(see the synonym table), and the jury scene carries numbers and procedure, not adjectives.

## Sense decisions made against the CSV

- **`die Leiter` is the ladder** (*"Haben Sie eine Leiter für mich?"*, CSV `die Leiter, -n`),
  Text 8. The manager (`der Leiter / die Leiterin`) is a separate entry owned by Batch 14;
  *Leiterin* is never written here. Gloss for the carding pass: *ladder — pl. die Leitern*.
- **`knapp`** is CSV sense 3–5, *only just* (*"knapp gewonnen"*), Text 9.
- **`echt`** is CSV sense 1, *genuine* (*"echtes Leder"*), Text 5. The filler *echt?* is kept
  out of Texts 1–4.
- **`einfach`** lands as sense 3 (*einfach toll*-type), wherever the scan sees it first.
- **`das Mal`** is the occasion (*"zum ersten Mal"*, *"nächstes Mal"*), never *mal*.
- **`das Mittel`** is CSV sense 1, a remedy (*"ein Mittel gegen Mücken"*), Text 14. Not a
  compound: the scanner does not credit *Mückenmittel*.
- **`der Wert`** is sense 1, monetary value; **`wert`** is *worth* (*"Die ist viel wert"*);
  both in Text 15 with `wertvoll`/`wertlos` as one derivational family.
- **`das Original` / `original`**: the original against a copy, Text 15. Kept apart from
  `echt` (Text 5).
- **`die Höhe` / `die Breite`** are the CSV's furniture measurements (Text 5).
- **`das Tempo`** is pace, not the speed-limit sign (*"in diesem Tempo"*), Text 13.
- **`regional`** is regional produce (*"Sind die regional?"*), Text 2; the CSV only shows
  *Regionalbahn*, which belongs to transport.
- **`unheimlich`** is CSV sense 1–3, *eerie* (Text 14); **`wahnsinnig`** is sense 1, *Bist du
  wahnsinnig?* (Text 6); **`unglaublich`** is the intensifier (Text 6).
- **`pauschal`** is the flat-rate sense of the CSV (*pauschal gebucht*): delivery *"pauschal
  bis zur Haustür"*, Text 6.
- **`Groß-`** is taught through *Großmutter*, **`Einzel-`** through *Einzelstück*; both need a
  manual `use`.
- **`gewohnt`** is the adjective (*"Ich bin es gewohnt, …"*). The Perfekt *gewohnt* of
  *wohnen* is banned batch-wide because the scanner would credit it.

Decisions made as a result:

- **Why a new sofa:** the old one is the previous tenant's (B1) and *"seit gestern ist eine
  Feder kaputt"*. It is never named again after Text 6; the new one goes back.
- **The sofa does not fit**: 2.20 m long, and it will not go round the turn of the 80 cm stairs. It stays in the courtyard
  until the Hoffest, where the jury sits on it, and is collected on the Monday. Julia keeps
  her old sofa.
- **Neumann says Sie to Leon** (*"Herr Brandt"*), as he does to everyone — the joke of
  Text 9. Leon ↔ Jule du.
- No dates with weekdays; the calendar is relative (Y4 weekdays are not fixed).
- No money talk beyond the price of the sofa and a market price (B12 owns affordability).
- No school, no exams for Leon or Jule (B15); no holiday plans (B22); no job detail (B14).
  Leon's age, the cat's name and colour stay unstated.

## New standing facts

- **Herr Neumann's Hoffest with a Kuchenwettbewerb**, a Saturday in mid-July Y4, in the
  courtyard; lights in the pear tree. Rules: one cake per flat, registration with
  Neumann; the jury is *"eine kleine Gruppe, drei Personen"*. Jury: Neumann, Leon, Jule.
- **Bruckner wins** with a Marmorkuchen from his mother's recipe, baked with Tobi;
  **Frau Wolf comes second** with her grandmother's Kirschkuchen, 8.3 to 8.7: Leon gave every
  cake ten points except hers (nine, for the cherry stones). She thinks Leon was not neutral
  (he plays with Bruckner's railway).
- Julia bought a sofa at **Möbel Winkler** (saleswoman **Frau Winkler**); 2.20 m long, it did
  not get round the stairs and went back. Julia still has the old sofa with a broken spring.
- Tobi starts a diet after the Hoffest (an app plan, chili theory); nothing permanent.
- Bruckner bought an old locomotive at a flea market: a copy, worth nothing, *"für mich viel
  wert"*; he wants to **enlarge his railway** and finally build *"die zweite Brücke, über das Sofa"*
  (callback to B16).
- Jule knows the regulars at the café gegenüber; Frau Wolf is *"seit Jahren eine treue
  Kundin"*, comes *gewöhnlich* at three and always orders coffee and Apfelkuchen (as in B14 T5).

### Scene 1 — Der Aushang

- **Premise:** Neumann pins up the notice for the Hoffest with a cake contest.
- **Angle:** The registration form asks *männlich / weiblich*. Bruckner signs up, Frau Wolf
  laughs, and the contest becomes Wolf against Bruckner.
- **Words (10):** allgemein, erforderlich, einzig-, weiblich, gleichberechtigt, streng, die Gruppe, bestimmt, das Mal, besonder-

### Scene 2 — Auf dem Markt

- **Premise:** Saturday market, Frau Wolf and Julia buy cherries for the cake.
- **Angle:** Frau Wolf interrogates the cherries like a jury, criticises them, and buys five kilos.
- **Words (7):** regional, haltbar, die Qualität, verschieden, einzeln, reif, furchtbar

### Scene 3 — Kuchen für vierzig

- **Premise:** Frau Wolf's kitchen. Her grandmother's recipe is for twelve pieces; forty guests.
- **Angle:** She multiplies everything except the baking tray. The test cake is the size of a
  door.
- **Words (8):** die Menge, enthalten, ausreichen, der Bedarf, riesig, ideal, gewohnt, Groß-

### Scene 4 — Die Rezeptkarte

- **Premise:** Every entry needs a recipe card. Bruckner's reads like a Hausordnung.
- **Angle:** Julia corrects it; Bruckner defends every sentence; the one real mistake is
  sugar and salt the wrong way round.
- **Words (9):** deutlich, der Satz, korrigieren, stilistisch, umgekehrt, verwechseln, mündlich, die Einzelheit, ehrlich

### Scene 5 — Möbel Winkler

- **Premise:** Julia's old sofa has a broken spring. Furniture shop.
- **Angle:** Tobi chooses by sitting; Julia by measuring the room. Nobody measures the stairs.
- **Words (11):** bequem, die Breite, die Höhe, echt, die Größe, preiswert, teuer, hübsch, das Ding, gültig, verlängern

### Scene 6 — Pauschal bis zur Haustür

- **Premise:** Delivery day. The delivery man's flat rate ends at the front door.
- **Angle:** a 2.20 m sofa, 80 cm stairs with a turn (B6 T1). It stays in the courtyard — the jury will need
  a seat.
- **Words (9):** pauschal, kräftig, schwer, das Gewicht, steil, wahnsinnig, notwendig, die Schwierigkeit, unglaublich

### Scene 7 — Zwei Mechaniker backen

- **Premise:** Friday, Bruckner's kitchen. Bruckner bakes, Tobi helps.
- **Angle:** They bake like they repair brakes: scales, measuring tape, no tasting.
- **Words (8):** messen, minimal, flexibel, zuverlässig, praktisch, perfekt, schwierig, erfordern

### Scene 8 — Licht im Birnbaum

- **Premise:** Neumann hangs lights in the pear tree from a ladder.
- **Angle:** Neumann's own sign says two people for every ladder. Bruckner holds it and is
  needed again.
- **Words (6):** die Leiter, nötig, ausreichend, klug, einheitlich, nützlich

### Scene 9 — Die Jury

- **Premise:** The Hoffest. Jury on Julia's sofa: Neumann, Leon, Jule.
- **Angle:** Leon gives every cake ten points, Neumann calculates averages to one decimal
  place. Bruckner wins by 0.4, which is exactly Leon's one point.
- **Words (8):** zahlreich, gerecht, ausgezeichnet, eindeutig, der Durchschnitt, unterschiedlich, ähnlich, knapp

### Scene 10 — Zweiter Platz

- **Premise:** Evening, Frau Wolf's balcony; Julia brings the leftover cake.
- **Angle:** Frau Wolf is a gracious loser for exactly one sentence.
- **Words (7):** fair, schlimm, tolerant, schlecht, der Vorteil, verständlich, spannend

### Scene 11 — Der Plan

- **Premise:** The week after. Tobi ate the leftovers and has an app plan.
- **Angle:** Five kilos in a week, chili for breakfast; Julia does the arithmetic.
- **Words (9):** schlank, dick, intensiv, übertreiben, realistisch, optimistisch, stark, scharf, individuell

### Scene 12 — Der Test

- **Premise:** Bruckner's living room. Leon tests the cat with a magazine quiz.
- **Angle:** The cat fails every test and is declared a genius.
- **Words (9):** intelligent, die Intelligenz, die Fähigkeit, ungewöhnlich, der Gegensatz, durchschnittlich, positiv, gewöhnen, super

### Scene 13 — Wie immer

- **Premise:** The café gegenüber, hot afternoon. Jule waits tables.
- **Angle:** Frau Wolf announces she will try something new today, and orders what she always orders.
- **Words (9):** die Gewohnheit, gewöhnlich, häufig, voll, das Tempo, genügen, prima, treu, zufrieden

### Scene 14 — Am Fluss

- **Premise:** The hottest Sunday. Julia, Tobi and Jule at the river, near the ferry.
- **Angle:** Jule wants to jump from the rock; Tobi measures the risk; Tobi falls in.
- **Words (7):** tief, die Entfernung, wild, unheimlich, gering, das Mittel, schwach

### Scene 15 — Ein Einzelstück

- **Premise:** Bruckner shows Tobi his flea-market locomotive.
- **Angle:** Original from 1950, says Bruckner. Tobi finds the sticker. Bruckner keeps it.
- **Words (10):** der Wert, wert, wertvoll, wertlos, das Original, original, reich, vergrößern, der Gegenstand, Einzel-

## Scanner landings and manual fixes (after Stage 3)

`scan --apply`: **133/133**. Unowned band-5 words landed at their first natural use:
`gut` T1, `groß` T3, `einfach` T4 (*"Schreiben Sie einfach: …"*), `klein` T6, `schnell` T13,
`hoch` T14. Every owned word lands in its owner text, except:

- **`gewöhnen`** is credited to Text 3 by the adjective *gewohnt* (*"Ich bin es gewohnt"*).
  Owner Text 12 (*"Ihre Katze hat sich gut an ihr Leben gewöhnt"*), recorded with
  `use --batch 21 --text 12`; the scanner's Text 3 row returns on every rescan and is ignored.
- **`Einzel-`** is credited to Text 4 by *Einzelheit*. Owner Text 15 (*"Ein Einzelstück."*),
  recorded with `use --text 15`. Card from Text 15.
- **`original`** (adjective) and **`das Original`** both land in Text 15; the adjective's
  card line is *"Dann ist sie gar nicht original?"*.

Edits made to keep false positives out: *genug* credits `genügen`, so Texts 4 and 8 avoid
it (*rechtzeitig*, *eine ganze Kiste*); *Reicht … aus* credits `reich`, so Text 3 says
*"Ob das Mehl im Schrank überhaupt ausreicht?"*; *voll* is kept out of Text 8.
*Tief genug* in Text 14 comes after the owner (Text 13) and is harmless.

Glue pool: 325/326 (only the CSV typo `irgendirgendein` open). Nothing skipped.
