# Batch 18 — Kommunikation & Post · Szenenplan

179 target words in **sixteen scenes** of six to eighteen words each. The orchestrator asked
for about 13–15 scenes, and the writer brief allows up to 16 for very large batches. Fifteen
would have meant either a journalist scene of fifteen words (the interview and the printed
article are two different jokes) or a phone scene that carries both the hotline and the
lost train call. Each text has eight turns or fewer.

The story slot is **April–May Y4**. It is the batch that owns post, phones, letters and the
Hausverwaltung's letters. A light arc runs through it: Frau Wolf campaigns for the lift by
letter (T5), on the radio (T6), at a tenants' meeting (T7) and in the newspaper (T8–T9).
Nothing gets repaired, because only Batch 23 may repair the lift.

The topic has two hazards:

- **Written register in speech.** `hinweisen`, `mitteilen`, `das Schreiben`, `die
  Aufforderung`, `die Kommunikation`, `ankündigen` and `der Standpunkt` are letter and
  meeting words. They are spoken here only by people whose job makes them talk that way:
  Neumann quoting the Verwaltung (T1, T4), Frau Seifert from the Verwaltung (T7), and Frau
  Wolf taking a stand at that meeting.
- **A list of phone and post words.** The phone words are spread over things that go
  wrong: a radio alarm at five (T3), a radio call-in (T6), a hotline that misspells the
  name (T14) and a train call that cuts out (T15). The post words are spread the same way:
  a letter for the previous tenant (T2) and a parcel without a postcode (T13).

## Sense decisions made against the CSV, not against the corpus

- **`die Botschaft` is an embassy.** The CSV's only example is *"Das Visum habe ich von der
  Botschaft bekommen"*. Text 2: an Einschreiben from an embassy for the previous tenant.
  It is never "message".
- **`der Empfang` is confirmation of receipt** (CSV sense 1, *"Bitte den Empfang
  bestätigen!"*). Text 2: *"Den Empfang bestätigt nur der Empfänger."* It is not phone
  signal, which keeps it apart from `das Netz` (T15).
- **`empfangen` is to receive guests** (CSV sense 2, *"Die Gäste wurden sehr herzlich
  empfangen"*). Text 16: *"Seit sechzehn Jahren empfangen Sie mich mit Kuchen"*.
- **`der Vertreter / die Vertreterin` is a representative** (CSV sense 2, *Elternvertreter*).
  Text 7: *"die Vertreterin der Verwaltung"*. **`die Vertretung` and `vertreten` are a
  stand-in** (CSV: *"Herr Dr. Meyer macht für mich die Vertretung"*). Text 4: Bruckner
  stands in for Neumann. Neither is ever a salesman.
- **`der Sender` is a radio station**, **`die Sendung` a programme**, and **`der Hörer` a
  listener** (*"Die Sendung hat viele Hörer und Hörerinnen"*). Text 6. It is never the
  handset.
- **`der Zuhörer`** is the audience of a talk. Text 7. This keeps it away from `Hörer`.
- **`die Vorstellung` is a performance** (*"Die nächste Vorstellung ist um 15 Uhr"*).
  Text 6: the prize is two tickets *"für die Vorstellung am Samstag im Theater"*.
  `(sich) vorstellen` is introducing oneself (T7).
- **`der Ausdruck` is an expression** (CSV sense 1, *"Diesen Ausdruck habe ich noch nie
  gehört"*). Text 9. It is not the printout. `ausdrucken` (T5) is the verb.
- **`der Punkt` is the time** (CSV sense 1, *"um Punkt 20 Uhr"*). Text 7: *"um Punkt
  sieben"*. Game points (T10) are never called *Punkte*.
- **`das Netz` is the phone network** (CSV sense 3). Text 15: *"Kein Netz."*
  **`das Netzwerk` is a social network** (CSV sense 1). Text 11.
- **`der Rat` is advice** (*"Können Sie mir einen Rat geben?"*), not a council. Text 4.
- **`raten` is *Rate mal!*** (CSV sense 2). Text 15: *"Ratet mal, was passiert ist."*
  This keeps it apart from `empfehlen` (T13).
- **`die Stimme` is a voice** (*"an der Stimme erkannt"*). Text 6. It is not a vote.
- **`die Rede` is a speech someone gives** (*"eine Rede halten"*). Text 16, Bruckner's
  second mock speech (after Batch 17's birthday speech), for the repaired telephone.
- **`der Artikel` is a newspaper article** (CSV sense 1). Text 9. It is not the grammatical
  article.
- **`die Wiederholung`** is a repeat broadcast of the lost news (T15). The CSV example is a
  repeated exam, which belongs to Batch 15.
- **`melden`** is *sich melden*, to get in touch (CSV sense 3). Text 7: *"Die Verwaltung
  meldet sich dann bei Ihnen."* Text 11 repeats it.
- **`die Meldung` is a radio news item** (*"die Meldung im Radio"*). Text 3: the radio
  alarm plays the five o'clock news.
- **`der Hinweise`** is a CSV typo for **`der Hinweis`**. Text 11 writes *Hinweis* and
  *Hinweise*: tips about the missing cat.
- **`Ratschlag`** has no article in the CSV. It is *der Ratschlag*, plural *Ratschläge*.
  Text 11.
- **`die Brieftasche`** is a wallet. The CSV marks *Portemonnaie* as D/CH, and
  *Brieftasche* is standard German. Text 13.

## Scanner notes: which text owns which word

Every word is in the text its scene owns. The scanner records the **first** sighting, and
in eleven cases a noun or a homograph in an earlier text claims the verb first. The card
for these words is cut from the **owner** text in the last column. Each owner is recorded
with `vocab.py use --batch 18 --text N`. The scan row stays, because the ledger's key
includes the text number.

| word | scanner sees it in | as | owner text, card line |
|---|:-:|---|---|
| `schreiben` | 1 | the noun *Schreiben* | **5**: *"Ich will einen Brief an die Zeitung schreiben"* |
| `aufschreiben` | 1 | *Schreiben* + *auf* in the same turn | **10**: *"Julia, schreib auf."* |
| `bitten` | 1 | *Bitte* | **8**: *"Frau Wolf, ich bitte Sie"* |
| `klingeln` | 1 | the noun *Klingeln* | **3**: *"Dein Wecker klingelt seit zehn Minuten"* |
| `empfangen` | 2 | *Empfang* | **16**: *"empfangen Sie mich mit Kuchen"* |
| `danken` | 3 | *Danke* | **11**: *"ich danke Ihnen!"* |
| `der Dank` | 3 | *Danke* | **16**: *"Gott sei Dank."* |
| `raten` | 4 | *Rat* | **15**: *"Ratet mal, was passiert ist."* |
| `stimmen` | 6 | *Stimme* | **7**: *"Das kann doch nicht stimmen."* |
| `fragen` | 6 | *Frage* | **11**: *"und das ganze Haus gefragt"* |
| `zuhören` | 7 | *Zuhörer* | **16**: *"Julia, hören Sie bitte auch zu"* |

**Trap:** `vocab.py use … "schreiben"` resolves to `das Schreiben`, because it matches
the first lemma case-insensitively. The verb's row was inserted with sqlite directly, on
`word_id` of lemma `schreiben`.

Other scanner behaviour:

- **`vorlesen`** is matched on *Lies vor* (T9). It counts, but the match is loose.
- **`die Hörerin`** in T6 matches the variant inside the entry `der Hörer / die Hörerin / der
  Zuhörer`. T7's *Zuhörer* also matches that entry, but T6 comes first.
- **`das Wort`** is owned by T6 (*"kein Wort über Herrn Neumann"*). T10 and T12 repeat it.
- **`melden`** is owned by T7 (*"Die Verwaltung meldet sich dann bei Ihnen"*). T11 repeats
  it.
- **`ansprechen`** really is in T12 (*"sprechen Sie ihn doch selbst an"*). The scanner
  matches any *sprechen … an*, so check its row if T12 is ever rewritten.

### Scene 7 — Die Hausversammlung

- **Premise:** Early May, an evening in the stairwell or courtyard. Frau Seifert from the
  Verwaltung arrives half an hour late for a tenants' meeting. Two tenants are left.
- **Angle:** Her graph shows 100% satisfaction with communication. The survey had one
  respondent: Neumann. Frau Wolf demands a date. Bruckner offers to repair the lift
  himself, and his suggestion is "taken along".
- **Words (18):** begrüßen, (sich) vorstellen, der Vertreter / die Vertreterin, der Punkt, der Zuhörer / die Zuhörerin, der Vortrag, die Grafik, die Umfrage, die Kommunikation, stimmen, auffordern, die Information, der Standpunkt, sich beschweren, verlangen, vorschlagen, der Vorschlag, melden

- **Deliberately absent:** `Rede` (16), `Idee` (11), `schimpfen` (3), `Auskunft` (13)

### Scene 8 — Die Presse im Treppenhaus

- **Premise:** Early May, the stairwell. Herr Sander from the paper has climbed to the
  fourth floor for his research and is out of breath.
- **Angle:** Neumann gives the press two minutes and *"in Planung"*. Frau Wolf gives it
  everything. Neumann's only firm statement is that conversations belong in the flats, not
  in the stairwell.
- **Words (10):** der Journalist / die Journalistin, beantworten, die Presse, behaupten, erzählen, die Recherche, informieren, die Visitenkarte, bitten, (sich) unterhalten
- **Chunks:** `Frage(Akk.) beantworten`, *"Darf ich Ihnen meine Visitenkarte geben?"* (CSV),

  der Presse informieren"* (CSV)

### Scene 9 — Seite zwölf

- **Premise:** About a week later, Frau Wolf's flat. Julia reads the article aloud; Tobi

- **Angle:** Twenty lines between the classified ads, with a dramatic expression Frau Wolf

  Erzählung."* She buys ten copies anyway.
- **Words (9):** der Artikel, vorlesen, die Überschrift, die Zeile, die Annonce, der Text, der Ausdruck, die Erzählung, der Schriftsteller / die Schriftstellerin

### Scene 10 — Sieben Buchstaben

- **Angle:** Tobi plays *okay*, which the dictionary allows. He then lectures Jan, for whom
  German is "a foreign language". Jan corrects him (it is his second language) and plays
  all seven tiles with *Sprache*.

### Scene 11 — Die Katze ist weg

- **Premise:** May, the courtyard. Bruckner's cat has been missing since the morning.
- **Angle:** Bruckner's handwritten posters against Tobi's neighbourhood network. The first

- **Words (8):** rufen, fragen, das Plakat, die Idee, das Netzwerk, der Hinweise, Ratschlag, danken

### Scene 12 — Richten Sie ihm aus

### Scene 14 — Mit zwei F

### Scene 16 — Du oder Sie?

- **Premise:** The Tuesday after Scene 14, Frau Wolf's flat. The technician has just
  repaired her phone, and Julia has waited with her all day. Bruckner climbs the four floors.
- **Angle:** He congratulates her on her working phone with his second speech in a month.
  At her birthday (Batch 17) she promised to call every morning, and then a week went
  by without a call. After sixteen years of Sie he offers du. They hug, and
  three lines later both are back to *Frau Wolf*. Julia points out that the lady should
  have offered. *"Gott sei Dank. Und morgen früh rufen Sie mich wieder an?"* Not a birthday:
  Batch 17 owns Frau Wolf's birthday (end of April).
