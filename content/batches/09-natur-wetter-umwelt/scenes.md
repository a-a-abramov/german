# Batch 9 — Natur, Wetter & Umwelt · Szenenplan

95 target words. **Fourteen scenes**, two to ten words each. The batch is large enough for
the skill's 8–14 range, and its words are seasonal. Snow, balcony soil, a heat wave and a
thunderstorm cannot share one afternoon. So the scenes follow the calendar from March to
July, and the weather words are spread over the months. There is no single forecast scene.

The topic has two hazards:

- **A list of landscapes.** Berg, Tal, Hügel, Dorf, Landschaft, Aussicht and Nebel could be
  recited as a tour. Instead they sit in one scene with something at stake: three hours'
  climb to a view, and the view is fog (Text 9).
- **Environment preaching.** Umwelt, Umweltschutz, Umweltverschmutzung, Abgase,
  verschmutzen and entsorgen are campaign words. Each is said by somebody with a
  practical, slightly selfish reason. Leon needs a school project, Neumann enforces the
  Hausordnung, and Tobi wants to get rid of a paint tin. Frau Wolf, the interviewee, misses
  the lorries.

## Sense decisions made against the CSV, not against the corpus

- **`der Kanal` is a TV channel**, the CSV's only example (*"über 30 Fernsehkanäle"*), not a
  waterway. Text 2: Frau Wolf saw the Wetterbericht *"auf dem dritten Kanal"*.
- **`die Birne` is the pear** (*"Die Birnen sind heute besonders schön"*), never a light
  bulb. Text 4: the pear tree in the garden.
- **`der Strom` is electricity** (*"Strom sparen"*), never a current. Text 5: the stairwell
  light on a thirty-second timer.
- **`die Energie` is personal energy**, CSV sense 2 (*"Abends habe ich keine Energie
  mehr"*). This keeps it away from `Strom`. Text 9: Tobi on the way up.
- **`die Erde` is potting soil** (*"Die Pflanzen brauchen neue Erde"*). Text 3.
- **`das Feld` is a field of vegetables** (*"Auf diesem Feld wächst Gemüse"*). It is not a
  form field, which Batch 6 used. Text 10.
- **`das Rind` is beef as a product** (*"Fleisch vom Rind"*). Text 10: *"Rind aus der
  Region"*.
- **`die Region` is local produce** (*"Die Milch ist aus der Region"*). Text 10.
- **`die Fläche` is an area in square metres** (*"eine Wohnfläche von 100 m²"*). The text
  says the bare word: *"eine Fläche von dreitausend Quadratmetern"*.
- **`das Gas` is cooking gas** (*"Wir kochen mit Gas"*), on a camping stove in Text 8.
  *Gib Gas* belongs to Batch 10.
- **`das Öl` is heating oil** (*"Wir heizen mit Öl"*). Text 5.
- **`die Flüssigkeit`** is a liquid that must not go down the toilet. The CSV's medicine
  example has no home in this batch. Text 7.
- **`fließend`** is *fließendes Wasser* (CSV sense 2), not *fließend Deutsch*. Text 8.
- **`der See` / `die See`** are two words. *Der See* is the park lake from Batch 8
  (Text 13). *Die See* is the seaside (*an der See*, Text 11).
- **`die Frucht`** is the plural *Früchte* of wild berries by the path (Text 9). The ledger's
  separate entry `Früchte (CH)` means *Obst* and is hidden as Swiss-only. The text does not
  use it in that sense.
- **`die Aussicht`** is the view (sense 1), not prospects.
- **`der Schatten`** is shade (*"25 Grad im Schatten"*), in Texts 3 and 11.

## Scene order is ownership order

The scanner records a word at its **first** sighting, so no word appears before the text
that owns it. These avoidances cost something:

- **`heiß` is owned by Text 1** (*"einen heißen Tee"*, CSV sense 2). The scanner reads
  every form of *heißen* as `heiß`, and Text 1 is the only way to stop the first *heißt*
  of the batch from claiming it.
- **Texts 1–7 never say `Wand` or `Wände`.** The scanner reads *Wand* as a form of
  `wandern`, which belongs to Text 8. Text 14's *"durch die Wand"* comes after it.
- **Texts 1–13 never say `Wind`**, which belongs to Text 14. Text 12's thunderstorm says
  *Sturm* and nothing else.
- **Text 1 never says `warm`.** Text 2's *Wärme* would be read as `warm` anyway, so Text 2
  owns both. **Text 1 never says `Sonne`, `Wolke` or `Regen` either.** The snow scene has
  only the forecast's *sonnig*.
- **Texts 1–5 never say `Luft`** (Text 6), and **Texts 1–6 never say `leer`** (Text 7).
  Text 5 says *"Öl ist teuer"* and not *"der Tank ist leer"*.
- **Text 5 never says `fließt`.** *Strom fließt* is the obvious line for Neumann, and it
  would have taken `fließen` from Text 13. He says *"Ihr Ökostrom ist für Ihre Wohnung."*
- **Text 5 never says `Energie`** (Text 9). Neumann saves *Strom*.
- **Text 8 never says `Berg`** (Text 9), although the hut sits below one.
- **Texts 1–9 never say `Land` or `Feld`** (Text 10). Text 9's view shows a *Dorf* and a
  *Tal*, not fields.
- **Texts 1–12 never say `Stein`** (Text 13), and **Texts 1–13 never say `Meer` or
  `Strand`** (Text 14). Leon's school film is about the *Ozean*.
- **Texts 1–11 never say `nass`** (Text 12). Text 3 needs *feucht* and *trocken*, and gets
  only those.

Scanner artefacts to fix by hand after `scan --apply`:

- **`der See` / `die See`.** Both headwords are the surface *See*, so Text 11's
  *"an der See"* credits both. `der See` belongs to Text 13 (*"Jetzt ist der See
  offen"*) and is recorded with `vocab.py use --batch 9 --text 13 "der See"`. The scan's
  Text 11 row for `der See` comes back on every rescan and is ignored. The card for
  `die See` is cut from Text 11 (*"Bei so einer Hitze müsste man an der See sein."*), and
  the card for `der See` from Text 13.
- **`Öko-`** cannot match anything. Its variant keeps the parenthesis *(ökologisch)*, and
  *Ökostrom* fails the prefix rule. It is recorded with
  `vocab.py use --batch 9 --text 5 "Öko-"`. The card line is *"ich habe übrigens
  Ökostrom"*.
- **`die Nord-/Ostsee`** matches *Ostsee* only, so Text 14 writes *Ostsee*. *Nordsee* would
  credit nothing, and *Norden* would be read as `Nord-`.
- **`trocknen`**: the participle *getrocknet* is not matched, so Text 11 writes
  *"Die trocknet … in einer Stunde"*.

### Scene 1 — Schnee im März

- **Premise:** Early March, a Saturday morning, Julia's flat. Tobi is about to take her
  bicycle to football.
- **Angle:** Tobi believes his phone over the window. The phone says sunny and fourteen
  degrees, and outside it is snowing. He has read next Saturday's forecast. The bike still has
  no light (Batch 8).
- **Words (9):** das Wetter, die Wettervorhersage, sonnig, schneien, der Schnee, draußen, die Kälte, kalt, heiß
- **Chunks:** *"Bei dieser Kälte fahre ich nicht mit dem Fahrrad"* (CSV), *"Ich mache dir
  einen heißen Tee"* (CSV), `ein(e) schöne(r) Wetter` (813, here ironic)
- **Deliberately absent:** `warm` (2), `Sonne` (3), `Regen`, `Wolke` (2)

### Scene 2 — Die Schulter weiß es besser

- **Premise:** Late March, the front door. Bruckner is going out with an umbrella under a
  blue sky. Frau Wolf is going to the hospital to see Frau Sattler and the baby.
- **Angle:** The television against the shoulder. Bruckner's operated shoulder has been
  his weather forecast since the operation. Frau Wolf refuses the umbrella, and the first
  drop falls.
- **Words (9):** der Himmel, die Wolke, regnen, der Wetterbericht, der Kanal, bewölkt, der Regen, die Wärme, warm
- **Chunks:** *"Immer Sonne und blauer Himmel"* (CSV), *"Heute ist es stark bewölkt"*
  (CSV), *"Wärme ist gut gegen meine Schmerzen"* (CSV), `Regen kommen(t)` (277),
  *"Mit Kabel bekommen Sie über 30 Fernsehkanäle"* (CSV, the TV-channel sense)
- **Deliberately absent:** `Sonne` (3), `Wind` (14)

### Scene 3 — Zwei Balkone

- **Premise:** April. Frau Wolf is re-potting on her balcony and calls Julia over to the
  balcony opposite.
- **Angle:** She asks for advice in order to ignore it. Last year's tomatoes drowned
  (Batch 3), and she now blames the sun. While she agrees not to water, she empties the
  whole can.
- **Words (7):** die Erde, die Blume, die Sonne, der Schatten, trocken, feucht, blühen
- **Chunks:** *"Die Pflanzen brauchen neue Erde"* (CSV), `ein(e) trockene(r) Erde` (20),
  `ein(e) feuchte(r) Erde` (44), *"Gestern hatten wir 25 Grad im Schatten"* (CSV)
- **Deliberately absent:** `nass` (12), `leer` (7)

### Scene 4 — Der Rasen hinter dem Haus

- **Premise:** Late April, the garden behind the house. Julia comes to complain to Neumann
  about the sand on every step. Bruckner is lying under the pear tree.
- **Angle:** Batch 8's prophecy comes true. Neumann's winter sand is now dirt up to the
  fourth floor. Neumann answers with a new sign, and Bruckner gets round it by
  re-classifying the lawn as grass.
- **Words (7):** der Sand, der Rasen, das Gras, der Baum, die Birne, die Gegend, der Dreck
- **Chunks:** *"Bitte den Rasen nicht betreten!"* (CSV), *"Wir lagen den ganzen Tag im
  Gras"* (CSV), `unter liegen … Baum` (109), `Baum blühen(t)` (38)
- **Deliberately absent:** `Wiese` (13), `Frucht` (9), `Schmutz` (another batch)

### Scene 5 — Dreißig Sekunden

- **Premise:** End of April, the stairwell, dark. Frau Wolf is stuck on the third floor
  because the light went out.
- **Angle:** Neumann saves electricity with a thirty-second light in a house whose lift has

- **Words (3):** der Strom, das Öl, Öko-
- **Chunks:** *"Wie kann ich im Haushalt Strom sparen?"* (CSV), `Strom(Akk.) sparen` (81),

- **Deliberately absent:** `Energie` (9), `leer` (7), `fließen` (13)

### Scene 7 — Die dunkle Farbe

### Scene 13 — Am See

- **Premise:** Late July, the park lake that froze in February (Batch 8, Text 5).
- **Angle:** The same roles as Batch 8. Jule is the brave one again, and Tobi and Julia
  stay on the bank. This time she does go in, and she is back out after thirty seconds.
- **Words (6):** der See, das Ufer, fließen, der Fluss, der Stein, die Wiese
- **Chunks:** *"Unser Hotel war direkt am See"* (CSV), *"Er schwimmt ans Ufer zurück"*
  (CSV), *"Dieser Fluss fließt in die Nordsee"* (CSV), *"Die Kinder werfen Steine ins
  Wasser"* (CSV)
- **Deliberately absent:** `Strand` (14)

### Scene 14 — Pläne für August

- **Premise:** Late July, Julia's flat with the balcony door open. Julia and Tobi have to
  book August.
- **Angle:** Frau Wolf joins the conversation through the wall, as always. She talks them
  out of a beach and into the island where she and her husband went every year.
- **Words (7):** das Meer, die Nord-/Ostsee, der Strand, die Insel, der Wind, windig, der Hafen
- **Chunks:** *"Warst du schon mal an der Nord/Ostsee?"* (CSV), *"Ich würde dieses Mal gern
  auf einer Insel Ferien machen"* (CSV), *"Wenn du in Hamburg bist, musst du dir unbedingt
  den Hafen ansehen"* (CSV), `Strand und Meer` (83)
- **Note:** the holiday itself is Batch 11's.
