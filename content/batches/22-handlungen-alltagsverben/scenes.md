# Batch 22 — Handlungen: Alltagsverben · Szenenplan

204 target words, **seventeen scenes**, ten to fourteen words each. The batch is general-purpose
verbs, so the scenes need people doing things with their hands. This batch's slot gives it a
**moving day**: **Herr Lorenz moves out of the third floor**, which closes the separated-couple
thread from Batch 7, Text 8. Nobody from the main cast moves. The batch runs in order: the date is
fixed (1), the household is divided (2), a replacement sofa is ordered (3), the helpers' plan
is printed (4), and Frau Wolf collects for a gift (5). Then comes the Saturday itself (6–13),
Frau Lorenz's first Sunday alone (14), the new sofa's delivery (15), and two late-August scenes
that use the remaining action verbs (16 courtyard football, 17 the lake).

Seventeen scenes is one more than the brief's ~16, and that is deliberate. Sixteen would also
fit the 10–16 band, but the two scenes a merge would absorb, the lunch break (11) and the delivery
(15), each carry their own joke. Merging either would leave that joke in a dialogue built for
something else.

## Sense decisions made against the CSV

- **`der Halt`** — skipped. The CSV sense is a stop on a route (*"Nächster Halt ist am
  Südbahnhof"*), and this batch has no transit scene (transport belongs to Batch 10). The
  particle *halt* would also be scanned as it. The batch avoids the interjection *Halt!*.
- **`drücken`** is *to press* (*"auf den grünen Knopf drücken"*, Text 4). **Ledger bug:** its
  stored forms are those of `drucken` (*drucken, druckt, druckte*), so the scanner credits
  `drücken` on *Drucker*. Text 4 carries `der Drucker`, `drucken` and `drücken` together on
  purpose. The joke is *gedrückt* → *gedruckt*: Frau Wolf presses three times, and thirty
  copies come out.
- **`der Druck`** is the tyre pressure (*"Den Druck in den Reifen habe ich schon geprüft"*,
  Text 6), which is CSV sense 2. The scanner credits it on *Drucker* in Text 4; the owner is
  Text 6.
- **`abmachen`** is *to agree*, the CSV's only sense (*"Wir hatten abgemacht: …"*, Text 2).
  It does not mean taking something off.
- **`ausmachen`** is switching a device off (*"Ich habe ihn schon dreimal ausgemacht"*,
  Text 4), CSV sense 2.
- **`wenden`** is a vehicle U-turn (*"Wenden ist in dieser Straße verboten"*, Text 6). The
  street is one-way (Batch 10).
- **`treiben`** is *Sport treiben* (Text 7). **`bestehen`** is *bestehen aus* (Text 15); the
  exam sense belongs to Batch 15.
- **`der Beitrag`** is a contribution to a collection (*"jeder gibt einen Beitrag"*, Text 5).
  It is the nearest everyday sense to the CSV's membership fee.
- **`dienen`** is CSV sense 2, *als … dienen* (*"Zwei Jahre lang hat es ihm als Wohnzimmer,
  Büro und Schlafzimmer gedient"*, Text 14). It was his room after the split (B7 T8).
- **`die Stelle`** is a place on a wall (*"eine helle Stelle"*, Text 14), CSV sense 1.
- **`sorgen`** in Text 8 is *dafür sorgen, dass* (CSV sense 2). It is not *sorgen für
  Kinder*, which would be a synonym of `betreuen` in the same text.
- **`aufheben`** is *keep* (*"Die Rechnung müssen Sie aufheben"*, Text 15), CSV sense 2. It
  is split from `behalten` (Text 2).
- **`die Darstellung`** is the on-screen picture, read aloud from the fine print by the
  delivery man (Text 15). **`darstellen`** is the arguing-couple sense (*"Sie stellt es immer
  so dar, als ob …"*, Text 2).
- **`die Integration` / `integrieren`** go to Neumann, who uses them in self-defence
  (*"Ich bin integriert. Ich bin der Hausmeister."*, Text 16). They are Tobi's ironic register,
  not the political sense.
- **`verbinden`** is dressing a wound (CSV sense 1, Text 11); **`die Verbindung`** is the
  printer's connection (Text 4).
- Formal words go to speakers who would say them: `benötigen`, `beschränkt`,
  `verpflichtet` and `die Bestätigung` to Neumann (6); `unterlassen` on Neumann's sign (16);
  `zusammenfassen` to Frau Wolf as referee (2); `die Besprechung` to Frau Wolf, ironically,
  over coffee (5).

## Ownership and scanner artefacts

The scanner records first sightings. It also credits most separable verbs on the base stem
alone (*sage* → `zusagen`, *nimmt* → `abnehmen`, *vor* → `vorhaben`). Its first-sighting rows
are therefore only advisory. **The owner text for carding is the scene below that lists the
word.** Mismatches were fixed or recorded by hand:

- **Band-5 light verbs** (`gehen`, `machen`, `geben`, `stehen`, `liegen`, `stellen`,
  `bleiben`, `bringen`, `brauchen`, `tun`, `halten`) cannot be kept out of early texts. Each
  owner text below uses it in a clear line. The **position verbs `stellen`, `stehen`, `liegen`**
  are scanned first in Texts 2, 6 and 12; their owner is **Text 14**, recorded with
  `vocab.py use --text 14`. `machen` → owner Text 7; `geben` → owner Text 2 (*"Gib mir mal
  den Stift"*).
- Recorded with `use` because the scanner credits an earlier text through a different word:
  `ziehen` → 7 (scanned in 1 via *zieht … aus*), `kämpfen` → 9 (scanned in 2 via *Kampf*),
  `ändern` → 12 (scanned in 1 via *anders*), `der Druck` → 6 (scanned in 4 via *Drucker*),
  `weit` → 9 (scanned in 4 via *weiter*), `zugehen` → 13 (scanned in 8 via *gehen*),
  `pflanzen` → 14 (scanned in 13 via *Pflanze*).
- Recorded with `use` because the scanner missed them: `entgegenkommen` → 9 (*ich komme
  euch entgegen*), `treten` → 9 (*getreten*), `sammeln` → 5 (*sammle*), `wechseln` → 8
  (*wechsle*), `üben` → 16 (*übe*), `aufhalten` → 7 (*halten Sie sie … auf*), `aufpassen`
  → 7 (*Passen Sie auf*).
- `drücken` and `drucken` are both credited in Text 4, where both actually occur.
- Separable verbs in split position whose owner is right anyway: `ausziehen` (1, *zieht
  aus*), `zusagen` (1, *sage … zu*), `vorhaben` (1, *was vor*), `darstellen` (2), `zusammenfassen` (2),
  `einsetzen` (16, *setze mich … ein*), `stehen bleiben` (13, *bleibt stehen*).
