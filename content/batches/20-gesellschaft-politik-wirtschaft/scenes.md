# Batch 20 — Gesellschaft, Politik & Wirtschaft · Szenenplan

20 target words, **three scenes**, five to eight words each. Batch 4 (8 words, four scenes)
set the precedent: a small batch gets few scenes, and each scene must have a premise that
would survive with the vocabulary deleted. The topic's hazard is the **lecture**: *Wirtschaft,
Bevölkerung, Freiheit, weltweit* invite a speaker who explains society. So the politics and
the economy enter the house only through three everyday objects: an **election poster** at
the bus stop (1), a **ten-cent price rise** at the bakery (2), and **one of Neumann's signs**
at the cellar door (3). Nobody explains anything they don't have a personal stake in.

## Sense decisions made against the CSV

- **`die Pflicht`**: the CSV sense is *legally required* (*"Das ist Pflicht"*). Text 1 sets it
  against the moral sense on purpose: Frau Wolf calls voting a *Pflicht*, Bruckner corrects
  her (*"Pflicht ist es nicht"*), she insists (*"Für mich ist es Pflicht"*). The CSV sense is
  the one Bruckner states; the argument is the hook.
- **`egal`** is CSV sense 1 (*"ist mir egal"*), here *"unser Viertel ist dem egal"*.
- **`die Geschichte`**: neither the school subject nor a bedtime story, but the everyday
  *"hat eine lange Geschichte"* (a thing's history), Text 3. It sits between the CSV's two
  senses and is what a B1 speaker hears most.
- **`die Gewalt`** is CSV sense 2, *force* (*"Die Tür ließ sich nur mit Gewalt öffnen"*), at
  exactly that object: the cellar door. Not violence.
- **`der Gott`** is CSV sense 2, *"Gott sei Dank"*. No religion line.
- **`menschlich`** is the CSV's own idiom, *"Fehler sind menschlich"* (CSV: *"Fehler zu machen
  ist menschlich"*).
- **`der Ausländer / die Ausländerin`**: the feminine form is the one spoken (*"Ich bin keine
  Ausländerin"*), the masculine in the same exchange (*"auch als Ausländer"*). The joke is on
  Neumann's pictogram, never on foreigners.
- **`die Wirtschaft`** is the economy, said by the baker as the reason for a price rise
  (*"Das ist die Wirtschaft"*), not a pub.

## Synonym / near-pair check

The batch has no true synonym sets. Derivational pairs are split anyway, because three
texts leave room:

| set | kind | texts | ruling |
|---|---|:-:|---|

| `der Mensch` / `menschlich` | derivational | 1 / 3 | split |
| `der Ausländer` / `ausländisch` | derivational | 3 / 2 | split |
| `die Freiheit` / `die Pflicht` | contrast | 1 / 1 | together on purpose: the poster promises one, Frau Wolf preaches the other |

## Scene order is ownership order

- **No `Welt` in Texts 1–2** (Julia's praise in Text 2 is *"im ganzen Viertel"*, not *der Welt*).
  `weltweit` is not credited to `Welt` by the scanner (compound).
- **No `Mensch` outside Text 1** before `menschlich` in Text 3; Text 3 says *"versteht man"*.

### Scene 1 — Das Plakat an der Haltestelle

- **Premise:** Early June, the bus stop in front of the house. A new election poster.
- **Angle:** The smiling candidate is *der kleine Vollmer*, who broke two of Bruckner's
  windows playing football in the courtyard. Frau Wolf preaches voting as a duty; Bruckner
  will vote, and Vollmer has no chance with him.
- **Words (8):** die Freiheit, populär, der Mensch, egal, außerhalb, die Bevölkerung, die Pflicht, die Chance
- **Chunks:** `Chance haben` / *"keine Chance"*, `Hälfte des/der Bevölkerung` (116), *"Das ist
  Pflicht"* (CSV), *"Wir wohnen außerhalb von Berlin"* (CSV), *"Diese Musik ist bei
  Jugendlichen populär"* (CSV)

### Scene 2 — Zehn Cent

- **Premise:** Saturday morning at Bäckerei Aydin. Every Brötchen costs ten cents more.
- **Angle:** The world economy arrives as ten cents. Tobi's fix (foreign flour) fails; his
  protest ends in buying two extra before the next rise.
- **Words (5):** die Wirtschaft, weltweit, ausländisch, der Nachteil, zusätzlich
- **Chunks:** *"Unsere Firma produziert für den weltweiten Markt"* (CSV, here adverbial),
  `ein(e) ausländische(r) …` (Firma 68, Geld 32), `ein(e) einzige(r) Nachteil` (80), *"eine
  Stunde zusätzlich"* (CSV)

### Scene 3 — Das Schild an der Kellertür

- **Premise:** Late June. Neumann hangs a pictogram at the cellar door, which sticks again.
- **Angle:** A picture *everyone in the world* understands, and Frau Wolf doesn't. Tobi
  demonstrates the force the sign forbids; the door opens, the sign falls; Neumann thanks him
  for his cooperation.
- **Words (7):** die Gewalt, die Welt, der Ausländer / die Ausländerin, die Geschichte, der Gott, menschlich, die Zusammenarbeit
- **Chunks:** *"Die Tür ließ sich nur mit Gewalt öffnen"* (CSV), `auf der ganzen Welt`, *"Gott
  sei Dank"* (CSV), `für danken … Zusammenarbeit` (28), `ein(e) lange(r) Geschichte`
