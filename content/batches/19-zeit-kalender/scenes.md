# Batch 19 — Zeit & Kalender · Szenenplan

136 target words in **thirteen scenes**. Most of them are time adverbs (`bisher`,
`inzwischen`, `vorhin`, `übermorgen` …), and a time adverb only sounds natural when
something hangs on it. So every scene has a time problem: a missed appointment, a doubled
Saturday, a deadline, a technician who rings in the one minute nobody is home, a holiday
with nothing to do. No text recites a schedule. Bruckner's daily plan in Text 2 has two
entries and a punchline. There is no sequence chain (`zuerst … dann … danach … zuletzt`)
in any one text; these words are spread over Texts 3, 8, 11 and 13.

Story time: **May–June Y4** (weekdays as in 2027, following B10's Mon 20 July in Y3). Bruckner
has been retired for about a year and a half. This batch shows his retired routine: he
wakes at five, keeps a written daily plan, misses his first doctor's appointment, is never
bored, and makes tea at 22:30 for a camper in a thunderstorm.

## Floating words (owner = first sighting)

These band-5 words come up in any dialogue: `dann`, `jetzt`, `noch`, `immer`, `heute`,
`einmal`, `erst`, `erst-`, `gerade`, `kurz`, `nun`, `spät`, `weiter`, `wieder`, `die Zeit`,
`das Leben`, `bereits`. They are listed under a scene below, but the ledger credits them
wherever they first occur. Carding takes the clearest sentence. Early texts are not bent
to avoid them.

## Sense decisions (CSV)

- **`das Zeichen` is a signal** (*"wenn ich Ihnen ein Zeichen gebe"*, CSV): Leon waits for
  Bruckner's sign to start the train (Text 11). The road signs are `Verkehrszeichen`.
- **`fest` is a fixed time** (*"keinen festen Termin"*, CSV sense 3): Bruckner's
  *feste Zeiten* (Text 2).
- **`extra` is "specially, on purpose"** (CSV sense 2): Julia gets up early on purpose
  (Text 9).
- **`Doppel-`** is the CSV's *Doppelzimmer* (Text 7). Lowercase `doppelt` is its own word
  (Text 3).
- **`die Dauer`** is *auf Dauer*, "in the long run" (CSV sense 2), Text 9.
- **`der Start` / `starten`** are the start of the outdoor season at the café (Text 1),
  not an aircraft.
- **`schließlich`** in Text 10 is the argumentative *after all* (CSV sense 2), so it never
  doubles `endlich` in the same text.
- **`das Zündholz`.** The CSV headword has no region tag; *Streichholz* is a
  cross-reference and *Zünder* is the Austrian word. Matchboxes in Germany say
  *Sicherheitszündhölzer*. It goes to Bruckner, the oldest speaker (Text 13).
- **Written-register words go to people who talk like notices:** `voraussichtlich`
  and `die Anzahl` to Neumann (Text 10), `jeweils`, `sich ereignen` and `heutig-` to Frau
  Wolf on her archive (Text 12), and `die Frist` and `vorläufig`/`endgültig` to a student
  with a deadline (Text 8).

## Scanner notes

- A noun credits its `-en` verb (*Ende* → `enden`, *Dauer* → `dauern`, *Start* →
  `starten`, *Zelt* → `zelten`, *Frühstück* → `frühstücken`, *Donner* → `donnern`). Each
  pair lives in one text.
- `noch mal` is credited by any utterance with both *noch* and *mal*; owner is Text 7.
- `aufstehen` is credited by any *steh-* + *auf* in one utterance (*"Auf dem Aushang
  steht"*, Text 10); owner is Text 2.
- *die meisten* credits both `meist` and `meist-`; `meist` (adverb) is carded from Text 6,
  `meist-` from Text 10.

### Scene 1 — Draußen

- **Premise:** Saturday 1 May, 10:00, the café across the street. Herr Unger is carrying out
  chairs; Frau Wolf sits down on the first one, at twelve degrees. Julia passes.
- **Angle:** She has waited since October and won't wait an hour. Unger once opened in
  April; then she was the only guest outside.
- **Words (7):** die Saison, der Start, starten, früher, bald, gerade, nun
- **Floating here:** erst, erst-
- **Chunks:** *Saison beginnen/anfangen*, *Ende/Anfang der Saison* (corpus)

### Scene 2 — Fünf Uhr

- **Premise:** Monday 10 May, 6:10, the courtyard. Julia leaves early and finds Bruckner on
  the bench with coffee.
- **Angle:** Retired, he still wakes at five; his body was never told. His plan says Frau
  Wolf phones at nine. The phone rings at ten past six: she is up as well, and has been
  waiting every morning until nine.
- **Words (9):** aufwachen, aufstehen, der Tagesablauf, früh, normalerweise, längst, fest, regelmäßig, jedes Mal
- **Floating here:** heute, immer, jetzt
- **Chunks:** *aufstehen + um Uhr*, *an Morgen* (corpus)

### Scene 3 — Doppelt

- **Premise:** Thursday 13 May, evening, Julia's kitchen; the calendar on the fridge.
- **Angle:** Saturday has Oma Erna at two in Tobi's own writing; Tobi has football at
  three, "notiert" on a yellow note that is in the washing machine. Then Julia finds a
  third entry for the same date: their cinema evening.
- **Words (10):** der Kalender, das Datum, übermorgen, doppelt, notieren, die Notiz, diesmal, zuerst, danach, der Moment
- **Floating here:** einmal, dann
- **Chunks:** *in Kalender stehen/schauen*, *Notiz schreiben*, *notieren + Datum* (corpus)

### Scene 4 — Pfingstmontag

- **Premise:** Pfingstmontag, 11:00, rain. Julia and Tobi knock at Bruckner's door.
- **Angle:** The free day they longed for bores them by eleven. They come to a retired man
  for company, and he has no time. He puts them to work.
- **Words (9):** die Langeweile, sich langweilen, langweilig, der Alltag, alltäglich, dauernd, selten, neulich, hinterher
- **Floating here:** die Zeit
- **Chunks:** *vor Langeweile sterben* (corpus)

### Scene 5 — Einen Tag zu spät

- **Premise:** Friday 21 May, 8:55, Praxis Dr. Sänger. Bruckner arrives for his nine
  o'clock check-up.
- **Angle:** The man whose railway is always on time is perfectly punctual, a day late. The
  doctor is running an hour behind, so he gets in anyway.
- **Words (10):** verpassen, pünktlich, gestern, vorgestern, nochmals, die Verspätung, die Stunde, halb, spätestens, zumindest
- **Floating here:** das Leben, erst-
- **Chunks:** *verpassen + Termin* (277), *Verspätung haben* (CSV)

### Scene 6 — Jederzeit

- **Premise:** Tuesday 25 May, the stairs by the second floor. Anna Sattler, exhausted;
  Frau Wolf with her shopping.
- **Angle:** Frau Wolf will take the child "jederzeit", except mornings. Anna needs
  mornings. The afternoons, when Anna is home anyway, are Frau Wolf's.
- **Words (7):** halbtags, jederzeit, ständig, zurzeit, mittlerweile, bisher, meist
- **Chunks:** *halbtags arbeiten* (CSV)

### Scene 7 — Rechtzeitig

- **Premise:** Thursday 27 May, evening, Julia's flat, the kitchen a mess. Gisela phones
  Tobi.
- **Angle:** She wants to warn them *rechtzeitig*, so she calls before she rings the bell.
  She is at the bus stop downstairs.
- **Words (10):** sonst, rechtzeitig, bereits, Doppel-, die Übernachtung, der Zeitpunkt, noch mal, bevor, letzt-, der Schluss
- **Floating here:** wieder
- **Chunks:** *kein guter Zeitpunkt für einen Besuch* (CSV), *zum Schluss* (CSV)

### Scene 8 — Die Frist

- **Premise:** Tuesday 1 June, near midnight, Julia's kitchen table. Jule writes the paper
  due at noon.
- **Angle:** Ten of twenty pages, which is "half" and "a nice number". She takes breaks
  from her breaks and keeps files called *vorläufig*, *endgültig* and *endgültig 2*. Then
  the laptop restarts.
- **Words (12):** das Semester, die Frist, fertig, die Hälfte, die Zahl, vorläufig, endgültig, die Pause, zunächst, das Ende, enden, der Anfang
- **Chunks:** *Frist enden(t)*, *Pause machen* (corpus)

### Scene 9 — Nur kurz

- **Premise:** Friday 4 June, 7:05, Julia's flat. Tobi has been in the shower for twenty
  minutes.
- **Angle:** "Sofort" takes a quarter of an hour with Tobi. When he comes out, the hot water
  is gone too.
- **Words (12):** kurz, sofort, dauern, die Dauer, das Frühstück, frühstücken, spät, manchmal, lange, oft/öfter, extra, vorher
- **Chunks:** *Frühstück machen*, *auf Dauer* (CSV)

### Scene 10 — Zwischen acht und vierzehn Uhr

- **Premise:** Thursday 10 June, afternoon, the front door. Julia took the day off for the
  smoke-alarm check and meets Neumann.
- **Angle:** She waited six hours and went down to the washing machine in the cellar for one moment. The
  technician came then. But Frau Wolf vouches for the alarm: she hears it every time Tobi
  cooks.
- **Words (10):** vorhin, der Augenblick, voraussichtlich, nächst-, inzwischen, die Anzahl, meist-, endlich, schließlich, irgendwann
- **Chunks:** *in dem Augenblick* (CSV)

### Scene 11 — Achtung, Katze

- **Premise:** Saturday 12 June, Bruckner's living room. Leon brings road signs he drew for
  the model railway.
- **Angle:** Bruckner allows only real signs. Leon's "Achtung, Katze" does not exist, until
  the cat sits down on the bridge.
- **Words (8):** zeichnen, die Zeichnung, das Verkehrszeichen, das Zeichen, ewig, langsam, tatsächlich, zuletzt
- **Floating here:** weiter
- **Chunks:** *Zeichnung ist fertig*, *ein Zeichen geben* (CSV)

### Scene 12 — Zwei Kisten

- **Premise:** Tuesday 15 June, Frau Wolf's flat. Julia helps carry two boxes: every
  calendar she has kept, fifty of them.
- **Angle:** Her life is in the boxes, with dates. Julia's missed smoke alarm is already in
  this year's. The future is next year's calendar, already bought, with its first entry.
- **Words (10):** die Vergangenheit, damals, das Ereignis, sich ereignen, erleben, die Zukunft, zukünftig, heutig-, kürzlich, jeweils
- **Chunks:** *Ereignis des Jahres*, *Zukunft und Vergangenheit* (corpus)

### Scene 13 — Das Zelt

- **Premise:** Saturday 26 June, 22:30, the lawn behind the house. Tobi and Jule test his
  new tent. Bruckner calls from his window.
- **Angle:** Tobi promised an adventure, forgot the lighter again, and is first inside at
  the first thunder. Jule, who had doubts, stays out all night.
- **Words (10):** das Zelt, zelten, übernachten, das Erlebnis, das Zündholz, der Donner, donnern, plötzlich, nachher, anfangs
- **Chunks:** *in Zelt übernachten*, *Zelt in Garten* (corpus)
