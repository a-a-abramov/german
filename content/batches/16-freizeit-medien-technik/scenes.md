# Batch 16 — Freizeit, Medien & Technik · Szenenplan

213 target words, the largest batch so far. **Sixteen scenes** of 11–16 words each (eight turns per text), one
per text, running from mid-January to late March Y4. One word is skipped (`das Billett`, see
below), so the plan covers 212.

The topic has two hazards:

- **A catalogue of hobbies.** Sports, instruments, art forms and gadgets could each be
  recited as a list. Each scene instead has one object or event with something at stake:
  a TV that stays black, a noise complaint, a lost file, a bet, a viral video. The one
  scene that is a list by nature (Text 6, choosing a sport from a brochure) is built as
  Julia's catalogue of excuses, and every item gets its own excuse.
- **Print words.** `Metropole`, `Tourismus`, `Mobilität`, `Technologie`, `Studie`, `Medien`
  and `Veranstaltung` belong in print. Each sits in a mouth that justifies it: Gisela reads
  `Metropole` and `Tourismus` aloud from a Prospekt (Text 15). A modern football trainer
  says `Mobilität` (Text 3). Bruckner grumbles about *diese neue Technologie* (Text 5).
  Tobi quotes a `Studie` the way he lectured on nutrition in Batch 3 (Text 7). The radio
  presenter says `Medien` (Text 13).

## Sense decisions made against the CSV, not against the corpus

- **`die Runde`** is a lap (CSV sense 1, *"fünf Runden im Park"*): Text 3 laps round the
  pitch, Text 6 lengths in the pool. Never a round of drinks or a quiz round. Text 7, the
  quiz, avoids the word on purpose.
- **`die Figur`** is a person's figure (CSV sense 1, *"Lars hat eine gute Figur"*). Text 11:
  *"Damals hatte er noch eine gute Figur."* Never a character in a novel. Text 12, the book
  reading, avoids the word.
- **`der Virus`** is a computer virus, the CSV's only sense. Text 5.
- **`der Spiegel`** is a mirror (Text 4), not the magazine.
- **`die Station`** is a stop on the U-Bahn (CSV sense 1). Text 9.
- **`aufnehmen`** is recording (CSV sense 2, *"den Film … aufgenommen"*). Text 10.
- **`ausstellen`** is putting on show (CSV sense 1). Text 11: *"Die Galerie stellt zwölf
  Bilder von mir aus."*
- **`ausgehen`** is going out (CSV sense 2, *"Gehen wir heute Abend aus?"*). Text 8.
- **`das Programm`** is the TV programme (CSV sense 1) in Text 1. Text 4 reuses it in sense 2
  (software on a phone).
- **`der Plan`** is a city map (CSV sense 2, *"einen Plan von Berlin"*). Text 15.
- **`das Fest`** is CSV sense 1 only, a big celebration. *Frohes Fest* is Christmas and
  outside the slot.
- **`die Fotografie`** is a single old photograph. It is Frau Wolf's word (Text 16), and it
  keeps away from Julia's `Foto` (Text 4).
- **`der Maler`** is used for its double meaning. Tobi, who painted Julia's hall in Batch 6,
  calls himself a Maler, and Frau Lorenz answers that painting walls is not art (Text 11).
- **`Bio-` / `bio`.** *"auch wenn sie nicht bio war"* and *Biogemüse* (Text 9), as in the
  CSV's *Biogemüse*.
- **`Traum-`** is *Traumberuf* (CSV: *"Mein Traumberuf ist Feuerwehrmann"*). Text 13.
- **`der Karneval`** is written plainly, with no regional aside. *Fasching* (Batch 3) is kept out
  of Text 8.
