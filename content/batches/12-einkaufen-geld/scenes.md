# Batch 12 — Einkaufen & Geld · Szenenplan

126 target words in **fifteen scenes**, five to fifteen words each. The story time is
**September of Y3**, using continuity.md's calendar convention. Slots.md calls the same
month "year 2". No dialogue names a year. Julia and Tobi are back from two weeks on the
Ostsee (Batch 11). The holiday is mentioned only in passing ("seit dem Urlaub"), with no
details.

The batch has a spine, so it does not read as a list of shops. **Tobi is short of money.**
His landlord raises the rent (Text 2), his washing machine breaks (Texts 7–8), and he tries
the Flohmarkt (Text 6). At the end of the month he sits down with Julia and the
spreadsheet (Text 15). Around him the house does its own sums. Frau Wolf's free pears
turn out to cost more than a bakery cake. Jule opens her first account. Bruckner meets a
cash machine. Leon brings a statistic to his pocket-money talks. Bruckner sends his first
invoice.

The topic has two hazards:

- **Banking prose.** Zinsen, Bankleitzahl, Einzahlung, Gebühr and Überweisung are form
  words. Each one sits in a scene where somebody is surprised by it. Jule wants interest
  and gets a fee. Frau Wolf wants to pay a neighbour in cash, and the neighbour wants a
  bank transfer.
- **The salesman monologue.** Garantie, finanzieren, Kredit, Versicherung, Vertrag and
  Angebot are sales talk. Each salesman gets one pitch at most, and somebody from the
  house cuts it short.

## Sense decisions made against the CSV, not against the corpus

- **`die Bank`** is the bank with an account (*"Bei welcher Bank haben Sie Ihr Konto?"*),
  the CSV's `-en` entry. The ledger row shows the bench plural `¨-e` but sits in this
  topic. Text 4 says *Bank*, and no text says *Bänke* or *Banken*.
- **`der Gewinn`** is a **prize** (*"Der Hauptgewinn ist ein Auto"*), not profit. It sits
  with `gewinnen` at the opening raffle in Text 9.
- **`der Verlust`** is the **loss of an object** (*"Der Verlust meiner Uhr ärgert mich"*),
  not a financial loss. In Text 1 Tobi has mislaid his wallet.
- **`der Test`** is a **school test** (*"Du wirst den Test schaffen"*). Leon in Text 10
  says nothing about the subject or the school. **`testen`** is **to try out**
  (*"Testen Sie unsere Angebote kostenlos"*). Tobi tries out the flea-market stereo in
  Text 6. Product-review *"im Test"* is not used.
- **`die Kontrolle`** is a **border/customs check**, next to `der Zoll`. The coffee beans
  wait at customs in Text 9. **`kontrollieren`** is a generic check: Frau Wolf checks
  Bruckner's invoice in Text 13.
- **`die Einnahme`** is a **business's takings** (*"Unsere Einnahmen waren … höher"*).
  These are Tobi's stall takings in Text 6. **`einnehmen`** is the CSV's *take medicine*,
  and no scene in this batch has a home for it (skipped).
- **`die Anlage`** is a **stereo** (*Musikanlage*, CSV sense 1). Text 6.
- **`der Kasten`** is a **crate of drinks** (*"zwei Kästen Bier"*, the D sense), never a
  cupboard. Text 1.
- **`die Marke`** is a **brand** (*"Markenschuhe"*, CSV sense 2). Text 7.
- **`die Karte`** is a **cinema ticket** (CSV sense 3, *"Karten für das Konzert"*). Text 14.
  No earlier text says the bare word *Karte*.
- **`zählen`** means to **count change** (*"zählen Sie das Wechselgeld nach"*). Text 1.
  The scanner folds umlauts, so it credits `zählen` for any *zahlt*/*Zahlen*. Text 1 has
  both, so the scan row lands in the right text and needs no fix.
- **`die Aktion`** is a **promotional campaign** for the shop's opening (Text 9). This is
  close to the CSV's *"eine Aktion für Familien planen"*: it is something organised for
  people, not a sale price.
- **`die Einführung`** is an **introduction to how something works**, the closest
  equivalent of the CSV's course introduction. The salesman offers it with the delivery
  (Text 8).
- **`bitter`** is coffee, the CSV's tea example moved one cup over (Text 9). It was
  already used in Batch 3. It returns here as this batch's own row.
- **`das Benzin`** is petrol for Tobi's little blue car (Batch 10), as a monthly expense
  (Text 15).
- **`die Vermittlung`** is skipped. Both CSV senses, the room agency at the station and
  the telephone operator, are dated. A rental-agency line would be wrong for Germany,
  because the tenant no longer pays the agent.

## Scene order and the scanner

The probe run showed which forms the scanner reads:

- *Kosten* credits both `die Kosten` and `kosten`. *kostet* credits nothing. `kosten`
  therefore lands in Text 2, the first *Kosten*. Its card is cut from Text 3
  (*"Bei Frau Aydin kostet ein Birnenkuchen zwölf Euro"*).
- *Miete* credits `mieten`, *Vermieter* credits `vermieten`, *Rechner* credits `rechnen`,
  *Test* credits `testen`, and *Einkauf* credits `einkaufen`. These pairs live in the same
  text or in the owner's order, except for `testen`. Text 10's *Test* comes after Text 6's
  *getestet*/*testen*, so the order is safe.
- Bare *Karte* stays out of Texts 1–13. *Fahrkarte*, *Chipkarte*, *Kreditkarte* and
  *Versichertenkarte* do not credit it.
- *zahlt* credits `zählen` (umlaut folding); harmless, see above.
