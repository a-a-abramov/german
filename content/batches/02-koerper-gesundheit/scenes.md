# Batch 2 — Körper & Gesundheit · Szenenplan

128 target words grouped **by situation** into 12 scenes. The batch is dominated by ~30
anatomy words, which is exactly the shape that produces a vocabulary list with quotation
marks. The rule applied throughout: **a body part appears only where it is the problem** —
the knee buckles, the tooth is pulled, the throat is raw, the finger is in the door — never
because it is next on an anatomical tour. The examination scene is capped at four body
parts for the same reason.

Cast carried over from Batch 1, so the two batches read as one flat and one autumn:
**Nadja** (the new tenant), **Tobi** (the friend who helps and eats), **Frau Wolf** (the
neighbour from next door), **Herr Bruckner** (the caretaker). New this batch:
**Dr. Sänger** (the GP), **Herr Kern** (the pharmacist), **Frau Roth** (the dentist),
**Meike** (the nurse on Bruckner's ward).

The Batch-1 callback earns its keep: Bruckner's back and shoulder are wrecked from carrying
boxes up four floors of a house whose lift has been broken for two years.

**Standard German only.** No A/CH doublets, no regional-contrast lines.

---

### Scene 1 — Montagmorgen, achtunddreißig Grad
- **Premise:** Nadja wakes up ill and phones Tobi, who diagnoses her over the phone with great confidence and no medical training.
- **Angle:** He upgrades her from a cold to flu to something fatal in four turns, then offers to bring soup he has not made.
- **Words (16):** krank, die Krankheit, die Erkältung, erkältet, sich erkälten, die Grippe, der Schnupfen, der Husten, husten, das Fieber, die Temperatur, schwitzen, der Hals, die Nase, müde, die Besserung, der Kopf

### Scene 2 — Anmeldung in der Praxis
- **Premise:** The reception desk. Nadja has no appointment, the wrong insurance card, and forty minutes of waiting ahead of her.
- **Angle:** The nurse is unbeatable at the counter and completely kind about it; Nadja thanks her for the trouble anyway.
- **Words (9):** die Praxis, die Sprechstunde, die Krankenkasse, der Patient / die Patientin, die Krankenschwester, der Kranke / die Kranke, der Arzt / die Ärztin, die Mühe, die Untersuchung
- **Glue:** *Seit wann?*, *woher*, *falls*, *sobald*

### Scene 3 — Bruckner beim Hausarzt
- **Premise:** The caretaker's annual check-up, which he has postponed for three years. Dr. Sänger listens to his chest and works out that everything wrong with him comes from the stairs.
- **Angle:** He is proud of being strong and admits nothing; she prescribes a lift he cannot fix.
- **Words (17):** untersuchen, die Untersuchung, atmen, der Atem, die Brust, das Herz, das Blut, der Rücken, die Schulter, der Körper, körperlich, gesund, die Gesundheit, die Kraft, taub, das Ohr, blind
- **Note:** four body parts only (Brust, Herz, Rücken, Schulter); Ohr and Auge appear because he mishears and squints, not as anatomy.

### Scene 4 — In der Apotheke
- **Premise:** Herr Kern hands over three things, two of which Nadja did not ask for, and explains which one must never be swallowed.
- **Angle:** The ointment is *nur äußerlich* — he says it three times because everybody gets it wrong once.
- **Words (11):** die Apotheke, das Medikament, die Tablette, die Salbe, äußerlich, mild, das Schmerzmittel, verschreiben, die Medizin, das Vitamin, das Pflaster

### Scene 5 — Tobi beim Zahnarzt
- **Premise:** Tobi, a grown man, in the dentist's chair. Frau Roth is fast, cheerful, and holds the forceps where he can see them.
- **Angle:** He announces how brave he is, at length, in a mouth that cannot close.
- **Words (11):** der Zahn, der Mund, die Lippe, die Zange, die Spritze, der Schmerz, beißen, bluten, mutig, der Mut, stechen

### Scene 6 — Der Sturz im Treppenhaus
- **Premise:** Bruckner goes down the last four stairs with a box. Nadja finds him; Tobi calls the ambulance and describes the address wrong.
- **Angle:** Bruckner insists he is fine while bleeding onto the stairs he himself cleaned this morning.
- **Words (15):** (sich) verletzen, die Verletzung, die Wunde, bluten, der Knochen, das Knie, das Bein, der Fuß, der Arm, der Finger, die Hand, erschrecken, der Krankenwagen, die Notaufnahme, die Infektion

### Scene 7 — Besuch auf Station drei
- **Premise:** Nadja and Tobi visit Bruckner after the operation. Meike the nurse runs the room; Bruckner runs nothing for the first time in twenty years.
- **Angle:** He has already worked out what is broken on the ward and wants tools.
- **Words (12):** die Klinik, das Krankenhaus, die Operation, operieren, der Pfleger / die Pflegerin, der Krankenpfleger, pflegen, die Therapie, leiden, spüren, das Gefühl, die Besserung
- **Note:** *pflegen* is used here in the ward sense; Scene 12 uses it again of Frau Wolf's husband. Card it from this scene.

### Scene 8 — Verlaufen im Wald
- **Premise:** A short Sunday walk that Tobi has "done a hundred times". Three hours later they are somewhere else entirely, out of water.
- **Angle:** He refuses to admit they are lost until the same bench appears for the second time.
- **Words (14):** sich verlaufen, der Muskel, erschöpft, der Durst, durstig, der Hunger, hungrig, (sich) ausruhen, die Erholung, sich erholen, die Haut, stechen, müde, schwitzen
- **Note:** *stechen* here is the mosquito; Scene 5 has the needle. Card it from here.

### Scene 9 — Frau Wolfs neue Diät
- **Premise:** Coffee on the landing. Frau Wolf is on day two of a strict diet and has brought cake to celebrate.
- **Angle:** She has replaced one addiction with another and considers this progress.
- **Words (9):** die Diät, abnehmen, mager, der Appetit, der Bauch, der Magen, die Sucht, süchtig, hungrig

### Scene 10 — Das Bad, Viertel nach sieben
- **Premise:** One bathroom, two people, one of whom is shaving off a beard he has had for four years.
- **Angle:** Nadja doesn't recognise the face that comes out, and says so.
- **Words (10):** die Dusche, (sich) duschen, (sich) rasieren, der Bart, die Seife, die Zahncreme/-pasta, das Gesicht, das Haar, der Kopf, der Blick

### Scene 11 — Zehn Minuten vor dem Vorstellungsgespräch
- **Premise:** Nadja in a stairwell before a job interview, hands shaking. Tobi talks her down badly.
- **Angle:** "Sei einfach du selbst" is the least calming sentence in the language, and he says it twice.
- **Words (11):** der Nerv, nervös, der Mut, mutig, das Gefühl, spüren, die Träne, der Blick, die Hand, das Auge, das Herz
- **Note:** *das Herz* first occurs medically in Scene 3; card it from Scene 3. *das Gefühl*, *spüren* first occur in Scene 7; card those from Scene 7. *mutig*, *der Mut* are Scene 5's; card them there.

### Scene 12 — Frau Wolf über ihren Mann
- **Premise:** Late evening on the landing. Frau Wolf tells Nadja, plainly and without drama, about the years she nursed her husband.
- **Angle:** No comedy here. It is the one quiet text in the batch, and it is short.
- **Words (6):** der Tod, tot, sterben, leiden, vermissen, pflegen

## Where the cards get cut from

`scan` records **first occurrence**, and a handful of words turned up in an earlier
dialogue than the scene that owns them. For those, the carding pass uses the text below,
not the scene above — one answer, no ambiguity.

| word | planned scene | cut the cloze from |
|---|---|---|
| die Untersuchung | 3 | **Text 2** (Nadja at the desk) |
| der Arm | 6 | **Text 3** (Bruckner rolls up his sleeve) |
| der Kopf | 10 | **Text 1** (heavy head, day one of the flu) |
| die Lippe | 5 | **Text 4** (the ointment is for the lips) |
| der Finger | 6 | **Text 4** (cut finger, asking for a plaster) |
| bluten | 6 | **Text 5** (the extraction bleeds) |
| müde, schwitzen | 8 | **Text 1** |
| hungrig | 9 | **Text 8** (too thirsty to be hungry) |
| das Gefühl, spüren | 11 | **Text 7** (the leg after the operation) |
| das Herz | 11 | **Text 3** (the doctor listens to it) |
| die Hand | 11 | **Text 6** (helping Bruckner up) |
| der Mut, mutig | 11 | **Text 5** (Tobi announces his courage) |
| der Blick | 11 | **Text 10** (Nadja's face at the beard) |
| stechen | 8 | **Text 8** (the mosquitoes) — the ledger was set by hand; Text 5's "die Spritze sticht" is the earlier occurrence but the weaker cloze |
| erschrecken | 6 | **Text 6** — scanner misses the strong form *erschrocken*; set by hand |

Everything else is carded from the scene listed above it.

**Two scanner matches landed on the wrong token** — the texts are correct, but cut the
clozes from the real sentences: `leiden` matched *Leider* in Text 7 (use "Herr Bruckner
leidet hier vor allem darunter …"), and `pflegen` matched *Pfleger* in Text 7 (use "Sie
werden gepflegt, Sie pflegen nicht"). Text 7 carries three distinct words that look alike:
*der Pfleger* ← "sagt der Pfleger", *der Krankenpfleger* ← "Ihr Krankenpfleger sagt",
*pflegen* ← the last line.

## Skipped, with reason

- **das Asyl** — asylum has no natural turn in a Körper-&-Gesundheit dialogue, and inventing
  a third party's asylum status at a doctor's counter is not a line worth memorizing by
  heart. Belongs with Batch 13 (Stadt, Ämter & Recht).
- **das Suchtmittel** — bureaucratic register; nobody says it in a kitchen. *die Sucht* and
  *süchtig* carry the meaning in Scene 9.
- **der Tote / die Tote** — no speaker in this batch would use the nominalized form: Frau
  Wolf says *mein Mann*, not *der Tote*, and a corpse does not belong in the comic fall
  scene. Skipped rather than contorted.
