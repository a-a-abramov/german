# Story bible: Batches 01–10

> Snapshot taken before the cross-batch sweep. The "Contradictions" lists record what was
> found then; `sweep-decisions.md` records how each one was resolved or why it was left.

Source keys: `B3 T9` = Batch 3, texts.md, Text 9 (dialogue: hard canon, learners memorise it).
`B7 sc` = Batch 7 scenes.md only (plan, header or continuity table; never said in a dialogue, so a softer constraint).
`hdr` = texts.md header paragraph.

**Year convention.** **Y1** = the move-in autumn (B1–B2). **Y2** = the next calendar year (B3–B7, Fasching to December). **Y3** = the year after that (B8–B10, January to August).
**Label clash:** B10 sc and the coordinator call B10's July/August "year 2 of the story". This is the same period as Y3 here. "Year 2" only holds if you count story years from move-in (autumn Y1 to autumn Y2 = year 1). B10 comes after B8 (January, after the December farewell) and after B9, so it is calendar Y3. Pick one convention for B11–14.
Checks: the lift is broken "seit zwei Jahren" at move-in (B1 T1) and "seit drei Jahren" in December Y2 (B7 T12). Bruckner has been caretaker 20 years at move-in (B1 T1) and 21 at retirement (B7 T10).

## Contradictions and tensions found (not fixed)

0. **Year label (B10 sc and coordinator).** B10 sc calls 20 Jul to 6 Aug "Year 2 of the story". In calendar terms it is the third year: B1–B2 are autumn Y1, B3–B7 run Feb–Dec Y2, B8 starts the January after the December farewell. The B7 sc "about a year on from move-in" agrees with this. "Year 2" is only right if years are counted from the move-in date. B11–14 writers need one convention.
1. **Bruckner → Tobi address.** B3 T9 is Sie ("Das mit dem Fleisch war Ihre Aufgabe"). B6 T1 is du ("deine Platte ist einen Meter breit", "kauf einen runden", "hätte ich ihn dir gebogen"), and so is B10 T5 ("Deine Bremsen"). du is now the majority form. The B7 sc continuity table says "du to Tobi" and cites B4 T2, which has no direct address to Tobi. The repo's own table is also wrong.
9. **B10 sc internal (minor).** The calendar puts the drive to Oma (T8) on Sat 1 August. The "Decisions" list calls it "the Sunday drive to Oma Erna". The dialogue names no day. The same list says Batch 7 implied Tobi visits Oma alone, but B7 T9 shows him going with Julia.
10. **B9/B10 overlap (minor).** B9 sc dates T13–14 to "late July". B10 sc re-reads them as the days before 20 July, so the late-July lake and booking scenes sit before the commute week.
2. **Frau Wolf → Julia switches from Sie to du with no scene showing it.** Sie in B1 T2 ("Ihre Nachbarin"), B2 T9 ("Julia! Kommen Sie rein"), B3 T6 ("Julia, Sie ebenfalls"), B3 T8, B3 T12. du from B4 T1 ("Julia, da sind zwei drin. Du bekommst …"). The B7/B8/B9 sc tables cite only B4/B5 onward and present du as the standing form.
3. **The pullover.** B5 T1: her husband's pullover, "drei Löcher". B5 T7: it is at Julia's ("den Frau Wolf aus dem Keller gerettet hat"). B6 T2: tied to it by "den habe ich unten im Keller vor euch beiden gerettet" (and B6 sc says so explicitly). Yet it has only a coffee stain, no holes (B6 sc: "the sweater has a Fleck and nothing else"), and it was Frau Wolf's size ("denselben in Ihrer Größe").
4. **B1 calendar.** B1 is "one week" of the move-in (B1 hdr), and the heating dies "in November" (B1 sc 10). Text 7 is titled **"Der Frühjahrsputz"** (spring cleaning).
5. **B3 internal order.** In B3 T9 (first warm evening) Bruckner is already "seit der Schulter" off alcohol. B3 T12 has him in hospital after the shoulder operation, eating properly "zum ersten Mal seit neun Tagen". Read literally, T9 must happen after T12.
6. **Tobi and smoking (tension).** B7 sc lists Tobi as "does not smoke". B3 T10 has him step out for a cigarette ("Nicht privat").
7. **Bruckner's leg (minor).** Knee operated in Y1 (B2 T6–7). In B4 T4 he says "keine kaputten Beine". Plausible after recovery, but worth knowing.
8. **Plan-only facts that dialogues never state.** Jule is 21. Erna is 84. The nurse is called Meike. B7 T1 is set in the café gegenüber. Julia drove to the Altenheim. Bruckner was at physio in B4 T2. The Lokal an der Ecke closed. Bruckner's flat is on the courtyard side. The B7 sc continuity table treats some of these as fact; later dialogue may state them, but none contradicts a text yet.
