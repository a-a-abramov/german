## Rules for every parallel batch
- **Main cast stays stable**: no deaths, no breakups of Julia & Tobi, no engagements or
  weddings of the main cast, no main-cast character moving out of the house, no new
  pregnancies in the main cast, no hospital stays of the main cast — unless your row above
  owns it. Minor characters may have small events.
- **Do not establish facts another row owns** (e.g. outside Batch 14, Julia and Tobi go
  "zur Arbeit" with no details; outside Batch 15, Jule "studiert" with no subject).
- **The lift stays broken** until Batch 23. Neumann stays caretaker. Bruckner stays
  retired in the Erdgeschoss with his cat.
- **New characters**: give them names not used anywhere yet (bible-01-10.md lists the
  names in use). New surnames must start with your batch's letters, so parallel batches
  cannot collide: 10: any unused · 11: A–C · 12: D–F · 13: G–H · 14: I–L · 15: M ·
  16: N–P · 17: Q–R · 18: S · 19: T–U · 20: V · 21: W · 22: X–Z · 23: any unused.
  First names: check bible-01-10.md and pick ones not in use.
- Keep earlier address forms (du/Sie) exactly as bible-01-10.md records them.
